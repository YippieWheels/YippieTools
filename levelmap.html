Notes

Special items (including NPCs) are drawn as labeled placeholder boxes with approximate sizes, because the original sprites are not available. Text, shapes, art, groups, joints and triggers are drawn from their real data. Curve handles on art are interpreted as offsets from each vertex.

About the tools

Every action edits the Level Data text, so you see how each change is written. Size limits follow the game (5 to 5000). The brush without collision makes one art shape per stroke; with collision it builds a chain of solid rectangles.
