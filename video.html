Video → Happy Wheels

Turns a silent video into a level: a button plays it, triggers can add sound effects.
⚠ This program was made by an AI for experimental purposes. Expect bugs of all kinds.
Drop a video here
or click to choose

1 · Trim & speed

How to trim: drag the blue IN and OUT handles on the timeline under the preview (or move the playhead and press the buttons below, or keys I / O). Only the part between IN and OUT is converted.

2 · Look

Changing these shows a live preview of the frame you are on. Press Convert to apply them to the whole video.
The camera shows about 640×360 (the video, the button and the floor must fit). Art used ≈ (colors − 1) × frames.

3 · Playback

Small text labels under the floor (frame chain / sound triggers)
Add floor + spawn (new levels only)

4 · Sound effects

Move the playhead to a frame, click a sound to select it (it plays), then press Add. Each one becomes a sound trigger that fires at that frame. Sounds marked ⚠ have an unverified ID: the game may play a different one.
Play the sounds during the preview

5 · Level & position

Advanced options
If you know the action number that turns a trigger off, put it here: the play button then cannot be clicked again while the video plays. Only if sounds play shifted by one in the game.
